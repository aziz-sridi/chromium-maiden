# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use social platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, and X and want a calmer feed and a second thought before sending hostile messages. The extension works in the background while they scroll, comment, post, or reply.

## Product Purpose

Chromium Maiden is a browser companion that detects harmful incoming and outgoing speech. It shields incoming content without making the page unusable, pauses hostile outgoing messages before they are sent, and offers respectful alternatives that preserve the user's meaning. Success means protection feels immediate, predictable, and easy to override when the classifier is wrong.

## Brand Personality

Protective, composed, and lightly witty. The maiden feels like a capable companion with dry humor, not a mascot that constantly demands attention. Product language should be direct and human.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-product styling, decorative gradients, glass effects, excessive glow, emoji-heavy copy, noisy dashboards, nested cards, moralizing language, and childish anime-shop presentation. The interface should not pretend detection is infallible or production-ready when it is still experimental.

## Design Principles

1. Protection should arrive before disruption: prioritize fast local feedback and progressively apply deeper analysis.
2. Explain the intervention: show what was detected and make reveal, rewrite, or override actions obvious.
3. Keep the maiden in the margins: personality supports the workflow instead of competing with it.
4. Respect user control: settings are reversible, overrides are available, and uncertain classifications are presented honestly.
5. Spend attention carefully: the popup reports only useful status and controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal conformance target is required. Maintain practical baseline quality through readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, reduced-motion support, and states that do not depend on color alone.
